## Based on NEO

Scriby began as a fork of **NEO**, a word processor for authors created by
**[Hugh Howey](https://hughhowey.com)** (<https://github.com/hughhowey/neo>),
released under the MIT License. The editor, the library and shelves, the
saving and sync rules, the exports and the first interface languages come
from his work, and Scriby would not exist without it. Thank you, Hugh.

Hugh Howey's original copyright and the MIT License text are kept, as that
license requires, in [LICENSE-NEO-MIT](LICENSE-NEO-MIT).

## What Scriby adds

Tom's additions, licensed under [LICENSE](LICENSE), include the Scriby name and
identity and its icon, the redesigned interface and design system, the
floating panels, menus and icons, the resting bottom bar, the Companion
(language-model assistant, story bible and connection settings), the layout and
overflow fixes, and the changes to the documentation.

## Internal names that stay NEO

Some internal names still say NEO so that existing libraries and updates keep
working: the `NEO Library` folder, the app identifier, the update feed, the
settings folder and the `NEO …` cover-font names.

## Third-party components

Scriby bundles fonts, spelling dictionaries, Paged.js, hyphenation patterns and
other open-source software, each under its own license. See `licenses/`,
the `LICENSE-*.txt` files in `fonts/`, `print/` and the dependencies in
`package.json`. Those licenses are not changed by Scriby's license.
